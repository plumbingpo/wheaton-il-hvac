Keeping Your Aurora HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in Aurora

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen. A breaker that trips every few days, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. Those are early signs of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're an affordable repair. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We find the root cause instead of treating symptoms, so small problems stay small.
